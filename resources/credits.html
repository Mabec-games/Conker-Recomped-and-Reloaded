Conker: Recomped & Reloaded 1.0 by Mabec Games: github.com/Mabec-games/Conker-Recomped-and-Reloaded. The game is built from Mabec-games/live-and-recompiled (the launcher branch), a fork of Live & Recompiled.

Conker: Recomped & Reloaded is an unofficial, non-commercial fan project, not affiliated with or endorsed by Rare, Microsoft or Xbox Game Studios. Conker and Conker: Live & Reloaded are trademarks of their owners. No part of the game is included: it is built from your own disc.

The game
Conker: Live & Reloaded (2005) by Rare / Microsoft Game Studios.
Live & Recompiled, the native port this rests on, by giacomozama, with TGP482 (GPL-3.0): github.com/giacomozama/live-and-recompiled. This launcher builds a modified version of it (no in-game settings menu, a settings fix).
Xbox XBE Recomp, the recompiler and runtime, by TGP482 with Joshhhuaaa (MIT): github.com/TGP482/XboxXBERecomp.
xemu and Cxbx-Reloaded, references for the Xbox's hardware. The ConkerXbox60FPSWidescreen patch. SMAA by Jorge Jimenez et al. SDL 3, Dear ImGui, Vulkan-Headers, volk, Vulkan Memory Allocator, glslang, Zydis, toml++, miniz, FLTK and Roboto, under their licences (see THIRD_PARTY.txt in the licence texts).

The decensor mod
By nastys (Italian, French), Vaani (English) and Theconker64 (Spanish): github.com/nastys/conker-live-and-reloaded-decensor. Downloaded from their releases when you turn it on, never shipped with the launcher.

The launcher
By Mabec Games, artwork included. Built with Qt 6 (LGPL-3.0, qt.io), libarchive (BSD-2-Clause), toml++ (MIT), miniupnpc by Thomas Bernard (BSD-3-Clause) and OpenSSL (Apache-2.0). Room codes use the EFF Short Wordlist 2.0 by the Electronic Frontier Foundation (CC BY 3.0 US, eff.org/dice). When a host's router doesn't report its public address, the launcher asks Cloudflare's STUN service (stun.cloudflare.com). It ships Zig 0.16.0 (MIT, ziglang.org) with LLVM, Clang, libc++ (Apache-2.0 with LLVM exception) and mingw-w64 to build the game, and is packaged with linuxdeploy and the AppImage runtime (MIT).

Thank you to everyone above.
